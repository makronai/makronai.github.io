# DMCA Copyright Claim Submission Portal

⚠️ Before you proceed - this is a serious legal action. Submitting a Digital Millenium Copyright Act takedown request is not a casual “report button.” False, mistaken, or bad-faith claims can expose <strong>you personally</strong> to substantial financial liability, legal fees, and even criminal perjury charges under U.S. and international law. We strongly encourage you to resolve disputes directly with the content owner first.
