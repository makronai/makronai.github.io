## Tools

The team at Makron is placing efforts in creating the best online free tools and services possible. Here is a non-exhaustive tentative list of what we have to offer from our **tools** side:

- [Audio Tools](/audio-tools/): convert to other formats, compress, trim, adjust volume of audio files.
- [Image Tools](/image-tools): resize, transform, add effects, filters, convert to other formats, of image files.
- [PDF Tools](/pdf-tools): convert images to pdf, pdf to images, merge pdfs, split pdfs, rearrange pages, rotate pages, add watermarks, edit metadata, compress pdf files.
- [Txt to Md](/text-to-markdown/): copy-paste any content from clipboard to convert them into markdown format.
- [Teleprompter](/teleprompter/): import/export scripts, record yourself, practice using timers, produce videos.
- [Discord ID Lookup](/discord-lookup/): view profile details, view or download avatar, banner images, decorations, badges, etc. of any user by their ID (not username).
- [AI Checker and Humanizer](/ai-detector-humanizer/): paste text to detect if it was AI-generated, or use our humanizer to make it appear less convincing.
