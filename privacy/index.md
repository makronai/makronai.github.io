# Privacy Policy
as of 20th April 2026

By using our platform, products, services, you are automatically providing us with the following information (just like any other website out there):

- your IP address (that includes your ISP and country information).
- your useragent (that includes your web browser and device information).
- your consent/agreement to our [terms of service](/tos).

When you fill in a form on our website, or any of its products, services, or subsequent apps, you may or may not automatically submit the following information to us based on the context and requirements of the form:

- your real name
- your physical location
- your email address
- your social media usernames/links
- your national identification photos

In case you are not satisfied by letting us spy on such little amounts of your data, if you would like to donate even more information to us, feel free to [contact](/contact) us on social media or via email.

<hr>

We do not sell any data to outsiders because we do not have any persistent storage for them. All the advertisements you may find on our platform are based on sponsorships or involuntary promotions - not personalized for any user.
